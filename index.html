Yalnızca cihazınızda kalan veriler

Liderlik tablosu (Meter, Team-Cali)

  • Oyuncu kimliği: ilk açılışta cihazınızda rastgele üretilen bir kimlik (örneğin bb-…). Telefonunuzun donanım kimliğiyle, reklam kimliğiyle ya da sizinle ilişkili değildir.
  • Takma ad: kendi seçtiğiniz ad ya da otomatik “Player XXXX”. Liderlik tablosunda diğer oyunculara görünür. Gerçek adınızı kullanmamanızı öneririz; uygunsuz adlar engellenir.
  • Skorlar: oyun sonu skorunuz ile tur süresi (hile önleme için).
  • İsimsiz sayaçlar: uygulamanın kaç kez açıldığı, kaç reklam gösterildiği ve gösterilmesi gerekip gösterilemediği. Yalnızca tüm oyuncuların toplamı olarak tutulur; kimliğiniz ya da takma adınız eklenmez.
  • Bu veriler, liderlik tablosu hizmetini sağlayan Meter (Team-Cali) sunucularına HTTPS ile gönderilir. Sunucu, isteği işlemek için IP adresinizi teknik olarak görür.
  • Amaç: sıralamayı göstermek, hileli skorları ayıklamak ve reklam gösteriminin düzgün çalışıp çalışmadığını genel olarak izlemek. Bu veriler reklam ya da profilleme için kullanılmaz ve satılmaz.

English

In short

Balon BUM is a balloon-popping game. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. The game uses limited data only for the leaderboard and for ads.

Who is responsible

YousefHUT · Contact: yusuferen5500@gmail.com

Data that stays on your device

  • Settings (language, sound, vibration, colorblind mode, glow effect), your best score and game counters are kept in a save file on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Leaderboard (Meter by Team-Cali)

  • Player ID: a random ID generated on your device at first launch (for example bb-…). It is not linked to your phone's hardware ID, your advertising ID or to you.
  • Nickname: a name you choose, or an automatic “Player XXXX”. It is visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name; inappropriate names are blocked.
  • Scores: your end-of-game score and the run duration (to filter cheating).
  • Anonymous counters: how many times the app was opened, how many ads were shown and how many were due but could not be shown. Kept only as a total across all players; your ID and nickname are not attached.
  • This data is sent over HTTPS to the servers of Meter (Team-Cali), which provides the leaderboard service. The server technically sees your IP address to handle the request.
  • Purpose: showing rankings, filtering cheated scores, and checking that ad delivery works. It is not used for advertising or profiling and is never sold.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The game shows a full-screen (interstitial) ad every few games. Ads are served by Google AdMob and limited to family-friendly content (G).
  • Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Details: How Google uses information from partner sites and apps and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears on first launch. You can change your choice in the game under Settings → Ad privacy.
  • You can turn off personalized ads or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

Permissions

The app only uses internet and network state (for ads and the leaderboard) and vibration. It does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage.

Children

Balon BUM is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared data, contact us and we will delete the related records.

Retention and deletion

  • Leaderboard records are kept while the game is available. The weekly board starts over every week.
  • To have your nickname and scores deleted, email yusuferen5500@gmail.com with your nickname. We complete requests within 30 days.

Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish KVKK you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. Contact yusuferen5500@gmail.com for any request.
